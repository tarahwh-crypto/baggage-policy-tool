誰的行李政策適用這張票？Whose baggage policy applies to this ticket?

輸入旅客實際開票行程，本工具將依 IATA MSC 與 DOT FMC 判定邏輯，逐步推算應適用哪一家航空公司的免費行李額度與超額費率。Enter the passenger’s ticketed itinerary. This tool applies the IATA MSC and DOT FMC logic step by step to work out which carrier’s free baggage allowance and excess baggage charge apply.

本工具依據《Interline Handbook - STARLUX-PRP-005》、《聯航作業程序 Interline Handling Procedures - SOP-AOD-26-002》內容提供。Based on the Interline Handbook (STARLUX-PRP-005) and the Interline Handling Procedures (SOP-AOD-26-002).
1輸入開票行程Enter the ticketed itinerary

依「原始開票行程」順序輸入每一航段。售票航空公司(MC)請填實際開票所顯示的代碼；若該航段為 Codeshare 由其他公司實際營運，請填「營運航空公司(OC)」，留空表示與售票航空公司相同。Enter each segment in the order of the original ticketed itinerary. For the marketing carrier (MC), enter the code shown on the ticket; if the segment is a codeshare operated by another carrier, enter the operating carrier (OC). Leave OC blank if it is the same as the marketing carrier.

本表列出各航空公司「擔任 FMC 時」的判定方式，「免費託運行李額度」與「超額行李費率」、涉美加與未涉美加均可分別設定（自家政策 FMC／改採 MSC－取售票航空公司／改採 MSC－取營運航空公司／第一家營運航空公司／其他）。判定順序：涉美加行程依 14 CFR § 399.87 與加拿大 CTA，先認「整張機票第一個航段的售票航空公司（FMC）」是哪一家，再套用該公司在本表的設定並全票適用——不因其他航段出現別家夥伴而改判，也不因停留分段而重算 FMC（例如 AS 的「改採 MSC」只有在 AS 自己就是 FMC 時才會生效）；未涉美加行程則依行程中出現的夥伴（售票或營運航空公司）套用該夥伴設定。MSC 本身仍依 IATA Res 302 於停留點分段各自計算。codeshare 認定：該段行程中只要有任一航段的售票航空公司與營運航空公司不同，即視為 codeshare；若全段皆無（營運航空公司 OC 留空＝與售票航空公司相同），則不套用「依第一段營運航空公司」之 codeshare 規定，改依 MSC 判定。依據：《Interline Handbook》（STARLUX-PRP-005）、《聯航作業程序 Interline Handling Procedures》（SOP-AOD-26-002）。此表僅供查閱，如需新增或修改規則請洽 AOD 管理單位。「合作類型」欄：目前與 JX 有 codeshare 合作者為 AS／AA／EY，各有協議約定的判定方式；其餘航空公司（含未列於本表者）為單純 interline，適用 DOT／IATA 標準規則（涉美加 FMC、未涉美加 MSC）。This table lists how each carrier is handled, with separate settings for free baggage allowance and excess baggage charge, and for US/Canada and non-US/Canada itineraries (own policy FMC / MSC – marketing carrier / MSC – operating carrier / first operating carrier / other). Order of determination: for US/Canada itineraries, under 14 CFR § 399.87 and the Canadian CTA, the marketing carrier of the first segment of the whole ticket (FMC) is identified first and that carrier’s row applies to the whole ticket — it does not change because another partner appears on a later segment, and FMC is not recalculated at stopovers (e.g. AS’s “MSC” setting only takes effect when AS itself is the FMC). For non-US/Canada itineraries, the row of the partner appearing in the itinerary (as marketing or operating carrier) applies. MSC itself is still calculated per journey at each stopover under IATA Res 302. Codeshare test: a journey is treated as codeshare if any segment has a marketing carrier different from its operating carrier; if none does (OC blank = same as marketing carrier), the “first operating carrier” codeshare rule is not applied and MSC is used instead. 機場地區分類採 IATA 三大區（Area 1 美洲／Area 2 歐非中東／Area 3 亞太）及其下分區。機場所屬國家由線上公開機場資料庫查得（約 9,000 個 IATA 機場／城市代碼，另內建常用機場作為離線備援），再依 IATA「國家 → Area／分區」對照規則歸類；俄羅斯以烏拉山為界（約東經 60 度）分屬 Area 2／Area 3。若代碼查無資料，該段行程不輸出判定結果，請確認代碼是否正確。分區依 IATA Resolution 302 之 Area 3 細分：日本／韓國自成一區；中國（含港澳）、台灣、蒙古與東南亞各國同屬「東南亞」分區；印度、巴基斯坦、孟加拉、斯里蘭卡、尼泊爾等屬「南亞次大陸」分區。故如 TPE-BKK 屬同分區內航段，BKK-DEL 才是跨分區航段。正式作業請以 SOP 對照表為準。Airports are classified by the three IATA Areas (Area 1 Americas / Area 2 Europe, Middle East, Africa / Area 3 Asia-Pacific) and their sub-areas. The country of each airport is looked up from an online public airport database (about 9,000 IATA airport/city codes, with built-in common airports as an offline fallback) and then mapped by the IATA country → Area / sub-area rules; Russia is split at the Urals (about 60°E) between Area 2 and Area 3. If a code is not found, no determination is shown for that journey — check the code. Area 3 sub-areas under IATA Resolution 302: Japan/Korea is its own sub-area; China (incl. Hong Kong and Macau), Taiwan, Mongolia and the South East Asian countries are in “South East Asia”; India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal etc. are in the “South Asian Subcontinent”. So TPE–BKK is within one sub-area, while BKK–DEL crosses sub-areas. For official work, follow the SOP mapping table.
